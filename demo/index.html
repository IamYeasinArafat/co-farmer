How the four fit together

  1. First Conversation — the assistant turns one chat into a farm profile, a hardware plan, and an AI team of specialists, all specific to this farm.
  2. Signal Path — the hardware it just designed goes to work: sensors report, the Master Node waters the bed on its own saved ranges, and every reading lands in the database.
  3. The Daily Council — every 6 hours, the agent network reads that database and turns it into a new, checked plan for the Master Node to enforce.
  4. Today's Advice — the same plan, in plain language, on the farmer's own phone.
